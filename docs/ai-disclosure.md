# AI disclosure

If a language model helped write the script or a text-to-speech model spoke it, say so. Listeners, directories, and your own organization should not have to guess.

## Suggested show-notes line

> This episode was drafted with AI assistance and reviewed by the publisher. The narration uses computer-generated speech.

Edit the wording to match what you actually did. If a person re-recorded the script, drop the sentence about generated speech. If you only used the tool in `--dry-run` and wrote every word yourself, you do not need a model disclosure for the script.

## Suggested newsletter line

> This edition was drafted with AI assistance and reviewed by the publisher before it went out.

## What to be honest about

- **Voice.** Computer-generated narration is not the same as a recorded host. Do not imply that a person sat down at a microphone if they did not.
- **Facts.** The model does not know your street. Anything it added that was not in your brief should have been removed in the fact-check pass.
- **Free-tier processing.** On the Gemini free tier, Google states that content may be used to improve its products. If your brief includes unpublished personal information, do not send it to a free-tier model.
- **No fake authority.** Do not describe the show as "AI-powered investigative reporting" or claim accuracy the review step did not earn.

The fact-check prompt (`prompts/08-fact-check-safety.md`) asks the model to look for missing disclosures, but a person still has to accept the final wording.
