# Title and thumbnail ideas

Propose titles and thumbnail concepts for this episode of "The Portage Park Peep".

## Show
- Tone: Warm, practical, and neighborly. Calm on safety items. No gossip, no scare headlines, no invented urgency. Speak like a well-briefed neighbor, not a radio announcer.

- Audience: Neighbors, small shop owners, and anyone who wants a short, local catch-up they can finish with their coffee.

- Episode length: about 5 minutes

## Produce
1. Five episode title options, each under 60 characters. Mark one as the safe default.
2. Five YouTube / social thumbnail text options, each under 6 words, plus a one-line art direction (colors, a single object or place, no crowded collage).
3. Notes on what *not* to show: no identifiable private residences, no crime-scene imagery, no unlicensed logos or celebrity lookalikes.
4. If a written edition is on (on (newsletter, blog)), one newsletter headline that can share a cover with the episode art.

Keep concepts cheap to produce: a phone photo, a simple typographic cover, or a public landmark the publisher already has rights to use.
