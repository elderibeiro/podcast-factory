## Produce
1. **Title** — under 60 characters, clear, no all-caps, no fake urgency.
2. **Short description** (for Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Amazon Music, and YouTube) — 1–3 sentences, under 400 characters. Say what the episode covers. Do not invent a hook that is not in the script.
3. **Longer show notes** — a short paragraph plus a bullet list of segments, with sources named when they were given.
4. **Chapter list** (optional) — segment names in order, no timestamps unless they were provided.
5. **Chapters / tags** — 5–10 plain-language keywords, no hashtag stuffing.
6. **AI disclosure line** the publisher can paste into show notes.

## Rules
- Do not include personal email addresses or home addresses.
- Do not invent listener counts, rankings, or "must listen" claims.
- If the script asked listeners to write in, use a placeholder such as `[add public contact later]` rather than inventing an address.
