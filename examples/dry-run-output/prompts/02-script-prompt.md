# Script (section by section)

Write a complete narration script for "The Portage Park Peep". This will be read by text-to-speech, so write for the ear, not the page.

## Voice
- Hosts and voices: Narrator, role: narrator, voice: Kore, style: warm, clear, and neighborly, like a local Monday briefing
- Default voice: Kore
- Tone: Warm, practical, and neighborly. Calm on safety items. No gossip, no scare headlines, no invented urgency. Speak like a well-briefed neighbor, not a radio announcer.

- Language: English
- Target length: about 5 minutes
- Sign-off: That's the Peep for this week. This has been The Portage Park Peep. See you next Monday.


## Format (required)
- Start with a single title line: `# The Portage Park Peep — Podcast Script`
- Then write the spoken episode, split into sections separated by a line that contains only `---`.
- The first spoken section is the welcome. The last spoken section is the sign-off.
- Do not put host names in the welcome or the sign-off unless the outline explicitly says the show names its hosts on the air.
- Spell out dates, times, dollar amounts, and addresses the way a narrator should say them ("Monday, November twenty-third", "five p.m.", "forty dollars").
- Keep each section to short paragraphs (two to four sentences). A paragraph break is a breath.
- Do not include stage directions, music cues, or camera notes. Spoken words only, plus the `---` separators.

## Recurring sections
- Welcome (welcome, required, ~20s) — Short greeting, the date, and one line on what week it is.
- Lead story (lead, required, ~45s) — The week's one story worth opening with.
- This Week's Snapshot (snapshot, required, ~40s) — Weather, calendar, and one practical note for the days ahead.
- Commerce Corner (commerce, required, ~35s) — Shop openings, events, and neighborhood business news.
- The Mild-Mannered Mischief Report (mischief, required, ~35s) — Public-safety notes, calmly and practically, from official alerts.
- Homeowner's Huddle (huddle, required, ~35s) — Seasonal to-dos, city deadlines, and fix-it reminders.
- Park and Rec (park-rec, required, ~30s) — Park District programs, library or fieldhouse events, and things to do.
- Portage Past (history, optional / rotating, ~30s) — A short local-history beat. Rotate out when the week is already full.
- City Hall Whispers (city-hall, optional / rotating, ~25s) — Civic process, budgets, and street projects that affect the neighborhood.
- Neighborly Natter (natter, optional / rotating, ~20s) — Community groups, volunteer notes, and a light closer when it fits.
- Sign-off (signoff, required, ~15s) — One practical recap and a goodbye. No host names unless you change that rule.
