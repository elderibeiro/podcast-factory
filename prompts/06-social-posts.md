# Social posts

Write a small set of posts a volunteer could paste into Instagram, Facebook, Nextdoor, or a shop's stories for "{show_name}".

## Show
- Tone: {tone}
- Audience: {target_audience}
- Platforms the show itself lives on: {platforms}

## Script
{script}

## Titles already in play
{outline}

## Produce
1. One primary post (about 400 characters) announcing the episode, with a short list of what's in it.
2. Two shorter variants (about 200 characters) that lead with different segments.
3. A very short teaser (under 140 characters) for a story slide or pin.
4. Suggested on-image text (under 8 words) that would also work as a thumbnail title.
5. Alt text for a simple photo of a local landmark, storefront, or newsletter cover — describe the image, don't invent one that wasn't supplied.

## Rules
- No fake urgency ("you won't believe", "breaking" unless the brief used that word for a sourced alert).
- No invented hashtags that claim official status.
- No personal emails, phone numbers, or home addresses.
- Do not claim the episode is live on a platform unless the publisher has actually posted it there.
