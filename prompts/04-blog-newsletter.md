## Write
1. A headline and a one-sentence dek.
2. A short opening that matches the podcast welcome, without assuming the reader heard the audio.
3. The same recurring sections as the show, written as tight newsletter items. Use subheads. Link or name sources when they were provided.
4. A "coming up / practical notes" box if the episode had dates, hours, or deadlines.
5. A source list at the end, in the same order the items appeared. If a URL was not provided, list the source by name and mark the link as needed.
6. A closing line consistent with: {signoff}
7. A one-line AI disclosure the publisher can keep or edit, for example: "This edition was drafted with AI assistance and reviewed by the publisher."

## Rules
- Do not add stories that were not in the script or outline.
- Do not invent metrics, quotes, or contact details.
- Do not include personal email addresses, home addresses, or family information.
- Keep the whole piece short enough to print on a few pages or paste into a blog post.
