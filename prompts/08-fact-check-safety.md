## Check, in this order
1. **Accuracy.** List every specific claim (names, dates, times, prices, addresses of public places, vote counts, weather numbers). For each one, say whether the brief supports it, it is a reasonable paraphrase, or it is unsupported and should be cut or flagged.
2. **Inventions.** Call out any quote, number, metric, or "listeners already know" claim that did not come from the brief or outline.
3. **Safety and fairness.** Flag gossip, speculation about suspects or motives, unnecessary identification of private people (especially minors), and any wording that could get a small organization into legal trouble.
4. **Tone.** Note anything that sounds like an ad, a scare headline, or activist framing the show did not ask for.
5. **Disclosure.** Confirm the materials do not leak personal emails, home addresses, or family details. If they do, quote the line and recommend a replacement.
6. **Verdict.** One of: Ready with minor edits / Needs cuts / Do not publish yet. Then give a short, prioritized edit list.

If the brief is thin, say so. It is better to ship a shorter honest episode than to fill the time with guesses.
