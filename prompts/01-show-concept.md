# Show concept

You are helping a small organization shape a podcast they can actually keep producing. Be concrete and modest. Do not invent audience size, download counts, revenue, or press quotes.

## Show
- Name: {show_name}
- Tagline: {tagline}
- Niche: {niche}
- Target audience: {target_audience}
- Tone: {tone}
- Language: {language}
- Release frequency: {release_frequency}
- Episode length: {episode_length}
- Hosts and voices: {hosts}
- Recurring sections:
{sections}
- Platforms they want to reach: {platforms}
- Written / newsletter edition: {written_edition}

## Typical sources and inputs
Sources they already trust:
{sources}

Regular news inputs:
{news_inputs}

## Write
1. One-paragraph positioning: who this is for and what a listener gets in a typical episode.
2. A short promise the show can keep every week (or at the stated cadence). No hype.
3. How the sections work together, and which ones can rotate if time is short.
4. A voice and language guide: words to favor, words to avoid, how to handle hard news (crime, closures, politics) without gossip or speculation.
5. A listener-trust note: cite sources, say when something is unconfirmed, and never invent quotes, dates, prices, or attendance figures.
6. A closing line the narrator can reuse, based on this sign-off: {signoff}

Keep the whole answer under 800 words.
