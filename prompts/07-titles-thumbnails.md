## Produce
1. Five episode title options, each under 60 characters. Mark one as the safe default.
2. Five YouTube / social thumbnail text options, each under 6 words, plus a one-line art direction (colors, a single object or place, no crowded collage).
3. Notes on what *not* to show: no identifiable private residences, no crime-scene imagery, no unlicensed logos or celebrity lookalikes.
4. If a written edition is on ({written_edition}), one newsletter headline that can share a cover with the episode art.

Keep concepts cheap to produce: a phone photo, a simple typographic cover, or a public landmark the publisher already has rights to use.
