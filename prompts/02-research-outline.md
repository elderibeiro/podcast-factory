## Write an outline
1. A one-sentence episode hook (no clickbait).
2. A section-by-section outline that follows the show's recurring segments. For each section include:
   - Working slug or headline
   - The two or three facts that belong there, each with a source if one was given
   - Approximate spoken length in seconds
   - Open questions or facts still needed
3. Items that should *not* air this week (rumors, unsourced claims, off-niche stories, anything that could identify a private person without a clear public interest).
4. A running time estimate that fits {episode_length}. If the brief is too thin for a full episode, say so and propose a shorter rundown rather than padding.

Do not write the full script yet.
